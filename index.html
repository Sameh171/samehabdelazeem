About me

Where technology, business and people meet

I've spent more than 15 years in information technology — in IT management, cloud and enterprise technology, and digital transformation.

Over that time I've worked in IT leadership at director and CTO level and taken part in cloud initiatives across government and commercial sectors. Along the way I've earned more than 25 industry certifications, including AWS, Huawei, Oracle, ITIL®, PMP® and PMI-ACP®.

What keeps me interested isn't technology on its own. It's the moment technology meets a real business problem — and the people who have to make it work. That's why my work has grown beyond IT into business development, technology sales and training. All three are about the same thing: helping people see what technology can do for them, and helping them get there.
