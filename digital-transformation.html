Technology leadership

Digital transformation that people adopt

In my experience, transformation rarely fails because of the technology. It fails when the people who use it don't see the point, when processes stay the same, or when nobody measures whether anything improved. I start with the business outcome, keep the technology as simple as the problem allows, and bring people along from the beginning.

My approach

How I think about a transformation

Not a methodology — just the principles I come back to every time technology is supposed to change how an organization works.

  1. Start with the outcome

    Agree on the business result first — faster service, lower cost, less risk — and how you'll know it improved.

  2. Understand the process

    Map how work really happens today. Automating a broken process only makes it break faster.

  3. Keep the technology simple

    Choose the simplest cloud, data or AI solution the problem allows, and build on what already works.

  4. Bring people along

    Involve the people who will use it from day one, and train them so they trust the change.

  5. Measure and improve

    Track the outcome you agreed on, keep an eye on cost, and keep improving after go-live.
