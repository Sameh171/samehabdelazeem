My approach

How I like to teach

Good training should leave people able to do something they couldn't do before — not just remember slides.

  • Practical first Real scenarios, real tools and exercises people can repeat at work.
  • Plain language Technical ideas explained so that non-specialists can use them with confidence.
  • Connected to business Every topic tied back to a customer, a process or a decision.
  • Built around people Room for questions, different starting points and different career goals.
  • Young professionals
  • Technical teams
  • Non-technical managers
  • Sales & business development teams
  • Career changers
